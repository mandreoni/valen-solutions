COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions our clients ask most.

What does the free diagnostic include? expand_more
A one-hour call with a senior engineer to analyze your current problem, assess technical feasibility and propose a concrete path. At the end you get a summary of the bottlenecks we found and a scope proposal, with no obligation to hire.
Do you work with systems we already have? expand_more
Yes. There is no need to start from scratch. We analyze your existing infrastructure and software to integrate new features, modernize outdated components or fix specific performance or security issues without stopping your operations.
Who owns the code you build? expand_more
You do, 100%. All source code, technical documentation and configuration are delivered to your own repository. No vendor lock-in and no recurring license fees for the software we build for you.
Do you offer support after delivery? expand_more
Yes. Every project includes a warranty and post-deployment support period to ensure a smooth transition. We also offer optional monthly maintenance agreements covering continuous monitoring, updates and improvements.
